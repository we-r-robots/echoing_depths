# Idea backlog (not approved for building)
Ideas the user has raised that aren't specced yet. Each needs a design pass with the user before any work.

- **2026-10-05: PvP tournament mode.** A PvP-only, tournament-style mode without the PvE run (no encounters, guardians or Crystal), unlocked late in the story or after some time played, so players can keep enjoying the game through PvP. User direction: a **ranked, PvP-only mode with its own special rules**, team building by draft or shop (could be more traditional, close to Super Auto Pets). Its teams are **separate from the Vault's Echo pool**, unless we build an accurate power-level system that can gauge a team, in which case its teams could also serve as Echoes in the PvPvE mode.
- **2026-10-05: Summoner tile choice.** In formation setup the player picks the tile a summoner summons to; two summoners targeting the same tile level up a live summon instead of replacing it (see docs/design/class-roster-draft.md, 4b).
- **2026-10-05: Base-class Legendary at level 6.** A Legendary reachable by levelling a base class to 6 (in addition to one per advanced class). Parked for the future.
- **2026-10-05: Crystal of Remembrance rework.** The user isn't happy with the current fight: the memories are inconsequential and the fight lacks impact (the general idea stays). Ideas: **no Fading in this fight**; instead the Crystal has its own threats, e.g. an **AoE blast** on the party, or it **absorbs a party member's memory** and makes that hero fight for the Crystal; the party can damage their own hero, who returns to them in whatever state they're in when the Crystal loses control. Also fixes the payout problem (a defeat currently pays more than a victory). Needs a design pass with the user, then an update to 06-crystal-of-remembrance.md.
