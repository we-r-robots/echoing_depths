## Rejected
Freeshield, Rallier, Wayguard, Subduer, Hollowsworn A ("too iffy on the timing") and B, Spiteguard, Banneret, Charmer, Luckbringer, Salvewright, Hearthwitch, Embalmer, Mirage Weaver, Glyphwright, Stonereader, Ashcaller.

## Still open
- **Fighter Mercy+Order corner**: no pick (Lightsworn went to the quad). Unvoted: Vowkeeper, Truce-keeper, Aegisbearer, Lantern-bearer.
- **Rogue Mercy+Freedom corner**: Masquer maybe ("What happens if the Masquer is the weakest?"), Informant maybe (moved here by the user). Wispdancer A/B, Featherstep unvoted.
- **Homes** (q-1 "sort this out after this round"): Locksmith (not placed; q-2 "I didn't pick the locksmith", so Runebinder keeps its charge seal), Lanternbearer, Bountyhand (approved; the user asked what Assassin does before deciding).
- **Confessor flavour** (q-7): why does fire block or invert heals? Maybe "a retribution flame".
- **Wickburner name** (q-8): could stay; consider Emberheart if it fits the ability.
- **Enshriner rules** (q-9): a sealed foe still counts as standing. User concerns: re-sealing a tank you're trying to kill is annoying; does a sealed unit take Fading damage (if not, a sealed unit could win the fight); sealing the only front unit.
- **Ranged physical** (q-4): "Archers should be better in the back, and worse in the front, and they should do full damage everywhere, but may need their damage adjusted accordingly. Open to explore more ideas, research how similar games accomplish this."

## Gravecaller (q-3)
"I like Hold the grave, but I think that Nameless husk is the right option, if someone has died it should raise them instead." Build: if anyone has fallen, raise them (as now); otherwise raise a nameless husk of the Vault's long-dead in an empty front slot. Replaces the provisional Grave Bolt.
