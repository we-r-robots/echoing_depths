## Rulings (the 10 conflicts)
1. Hidden front units: melee skips to the nearest visible front unit; reaches the back only when no visible front unit stands. **Approved.**
2. Status damage: "Status effects are at the very least non-physical." Plus: "The back row might need reworked, what about ranged physical attacks? they should also be hitting for more damage." **Statuses non-physical approved; back-row ranged physical = open design question.**
3. Enemy swaps: in scope; "even open to breaking formation, just cautious at how powerful that may be."
4. Fight end: a unit at 1 HP is standing and wins the fight. Charge: no charge gain while the unit's own ability effect is active, or Fading damage doesn't build charge, so nothing goes infinite in the Fading.
5. Stun-lock: "Let's play test before adjusting" (no diminishing returns yet).
6. Summons don't count toward shapes: agreed.
7. Gravecaller: raised dead are weaker than the hero they were; needs a behaviour for when no one has fallen yet.
8. Stat budget per base class: approved.
9. Corner classes stay sidegrades: fine.
10. Names: adjust freely ("we can adjust names easily").
