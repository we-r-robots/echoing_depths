# Meta Progression: Lanternrest

## Design Principles
- **Variety, not power.** Meta progression widens a player's options rather than making their parties stronger, which protects async PvP fairness. Extreme corner classes may require light meta progression, so they must be sidegrades rather than stronger options.
- **No regrets.** No meta choice is permanent or mutually exclusive. Players choose the *order* in which things unlock, and everything is eventually reachable.
- **Progress on every run.** Wins feel special, but losing runs still move the village and the story forward.

## The Village Grid
- Lanternrest is a grid swallowed by gray mist, with only the central lantern lit at the start.
- As things are remembered, the mist pulls back, the village regains color and animation, and new plots open up at the grid's edges.
- **Customizable areas:** Players remember functional buildings and decorations onto any open plot. Buildings can be moved freely at any time. Each building has selectable style variants (e.g. stone, timber, or vine-covered) that can be changed later.
- **Fixed lore areas:** Lore NPCs and lore buildings appear in fixed story locations.

## Track 1: Shards (Rebuilding the Village)
- Shards are earned directly from victories or formed from Glimmers.
- Shards are spent to remember functional buildings, building upgrades, and decorations.
- Restorations form an unlock tree with prerequisites (e.g. the Blacksmith before the Forge upgrade), but nothing is mutually exclusive.

### Functional Buildings and the Preparation Screen
Functional buildings feed a **Preparation screen** before each run. Choices are made fresh every run and are never permanent. The number of slots is fixed; Shards only widen the selection. Later options are sidegrades rather than upgrades, each with a buff and a debuff.

- **Item Shop:** Choose one starting item. Upgrades add more items to the selection.
- **Adventurers' Guild:** Choose one Doctrine that shapes your classes for the run. Doctrines include **starting traits** that shift alignment:
  - **Hero-level traits:** shift one class's starting position for the run (e.g. "Healer starts at (0, 0)").
  - **Party-level traits:** shift every hero's starting position one step on one axis.
  - Starting traits are the main way to reach extreme corner classes. See `02-heroes-and-classes.md`.
- **Tavern:** Widens the pool of starting heroes offered at the beginning of a run.
- **Archive:** Houses the codex. Upgrades add in-run tracking of which advanced classes each grid region leads to.
- **Training Grounds:** Adds formations to the in-run pool.
- **Banner Hall:** Customizes how the player's Echo appears to other players (banner, title, palette).

## Track 2: Encounter Remembrance (Story)
- This track uses no currency. Encounters in the Vaults automatically remember lore NPCs and lore buildings into their fixed locations in Lanternrest.
- Triggers include:
  - Finding lore items or Lumari relics.
  - Encountering a forgotten person's memory as an event.
  - Reaching a Legendary class for the first time, which remembers that legend as a lore NPC. *(Tentative.)*
- Lore items are delivered to residents or the Archive to advance both the main storyline and each NPC's personal story.
- Because the story runs on discovery rather than Shard spending, building choices never block the plot.

## Track 3: Monuments (Victories)
- Every victorious party is recorded as a tableau in the **Hall of Remembrance**, a fixed lore building. Each record shows the party's heroes, classes, formation, and the Vault conquered.
- The Hall mirrors the Vaults: the Vaults record everyone who enters, while Lanternrest records only those it chooses to honor.
- A **monument plaza** displays a statue of one recorded party, chosen by the player and swappable at any time.
- *Possible feature:* additional pedestals as a Shard upgrade.
