# The Crystal of Remembrance
*Approved direction, 2026-10-05. Replaces the "Vault Heart". Amounts are balance, tuned in code.*

## Premise
Every Vault grows around a Crystal of Remembrance (see `01-world-and-lore.md`). The final chamber of a run is the Crystal itself. The party doesn't defeat it: they chip a Shard from it. As it cracks, it releases the memories it holds, and those memories fight to stay whole.

## The Fight
- **The Crystal stands in the enemy grid**, filling the two middle back-column slots. It never acts. It has a large **integrity** pool, and heroes attack it like any target.
- **Grid rules still apply.** Melee hits the front column first, so memories standing in front shield the Crystal from melee. Ranged and magic can reach the Crystal directly (e.g. back-first targeting). Formations and class choice decide how a party splits damage between the Crystal and the memories.
- **Chipping:** every 25% of integrity lost breaks off a **fragment**.
- **Memories surface:** at the start and at each fragment, the Crystal releases a **memory**: a unique enemy drawn from what it has stored. Memories take free slots in the enemy grid, front first, and fight like any enemy.
- **Victory:** the fourth fragment frees a whole **Shard**. The run is won, which matches the spec's "full Shard on victory".
- **The Fading still applies.** It is time-based, so a long chip fight erodes the party.
- **No retreat.** The party fights until the Shard breaks free or every hero falls.
- **Fragments on defeat:** if the party falls, each fragment already chipped becomes Glimmers. Glimmers form into Shards, so a run that got deep into the Crystal still moves Lanternrest forward ("progress on every run").

## Memories and the Story
- Memories are **authored characters and moments**, not generic monsters: a Lumari knight's last stand, a forgotten villager, the Lumari drawing memory from the world, the sealing, and eventually the Keeper.
- **The story progresses, not the difficulty.** There are no difficulty tiers, because tiers would split the async PvP pools. Which memories surface depends on the player's **story progress**: their remembrance so far, from Shards restored and lore found (Track 2 in `04-meta-progression.md`). Early runs meet common-folk memories; later runs reach deeper into the true history, in the order of `01-world-and-lore.md`'s "True History".
- **Horizontal, not vertical:** deeper memories are different, not stronger. Each brings new behaviours to answer (one shields the Crystal, one copies a hero's formation, one heals other memories, the Keeper dims the lantern-light buffs), so different parties and formations shine against different memories.
- **First defeat of a memory** records it in the Archive codex and can rewrite a codex entry ("Rewritten Codex Entries" in `01-world-and-lore.md`). Some remember a lore NPC into Lanternrest.

## Reward on Victory
- A full **Shard** (chipped from the Crystal).
- A **Remembrance**: the story memory released by the final fragment (it replaces the "Vault Heart memory" in `03-runs-and-combat.md`).
- A **Monument** record, as before.

## Floor Guardians
Unchanged: each earlier floor still ends in an authored guardian fight. Only the final chamber is the Crystal.
